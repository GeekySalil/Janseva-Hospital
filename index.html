About Us

Janseva Hospital & Trauma Centre Pvt Ltd.

A Legacy of Trust Since 1997 (Incorporated 2001)

Janseva Hospital & Trauma Centre has been serving patients since 1997 (Incorporated 2001) with a resolute commitment to affordable, accessible, and advanced healthcare.

Located in the heart of Varanasi, the hospital provides multidisciplinary medical services, advanced trauma care, complex surgical specialties, modern diagnostics, critical care (ICU/NICU), and proactive community healthcare initiatives entirely under one roof.
